# Art direction and generation record

Generated using the built-in `image_gen` tool, not the API/CLI fallback. Both final images are saved in this project's `assets/art/` directory. Graphite, uneven pen contours, crosshatching and ivory paper carry the visual style. The artwork is still for evaluation; the small loose bell, panel outlines and speech balloon are drawn with Bevy meshes and ink lines.

## `assets/art/panels.png`

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: background atlas for a 2D pencil-drawn comic game called Belling the Cat. Generate a wide landscape image, aspect ratio 3:2, split into exactly TWO equal width vertical cells side by side, with no gutter. Each cell is a complete separate horizontal-ish comic setting filling its cell, no external margins or borders, no lettering. Left cell: an old kitchen seen from the side at floor-level like a platform game stage, rough graphite and black ink crosshatching on warm ivory paper, vintage hand-drawn rotoscope comic mood evoking the pencil scenes of Take On Me. Window in upper left, quiet cupboards and floorboards, kitchen objects in background. Floor baseline at 80 percent down the cell. In bottom third leave an EMPTY walkable floor area with no foreground characters and no foreground bell; objects behind the stage only, a heavy sleeping cat vaguely visible on a bench in the far right background. Right cell: dramatic close-up of a large sleeping cat at bottom-left, eye peacefully closed, visible graphite whiskers and detailed fluffy fur, a small collar with a bell under its chin, quiet shadowy kitchen behind it. Leave the top 40 percent and upper right of this second cell pale and mostly empty so a speech balloon can be rendered in game. Style: confident yet scratchy unfinished pencil outlines, expressive black pen contour, visible construction marks, dry graphite hatching, slight hand smudges, no smooth digital vector, no photorealism, no colors beyond charcoal and off-white paper. Constraints: no mouse in either scene, NO text, NO speech bubbles, NO borders, exactly two equally sized separate cells. Game will crop each half as its own background.

## `assets/art/mouse-poses.png`

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: 2D game character sprite atlas, RGBA transparent background. Create exactly FOUR full-body drawings of the SAME tiny anthropomorphic field mouse in exactly a 2 by 2 grid of equally sized SQUARE cells. Canvas square. Each pose fully within its cell, generous transparent padding, no borders, no ground, no background, no lettering. Mouse faces RIGHT in strict side-view in every pose, wears a simple little scarf, round ears, long curved tail, expressive pointed snout, slender hind legs. Rough hand-drawn GRAPHITE and BLACK PEN, loose construction strokes, dry pencil crosshatching, monochrome warm off-white opaque body fill, vintage 1980s rotoscoped pencil comic look, not cute vector clip-art, not pixel art. Top-left: standing idle looking cautiously right, feet together. Top-right: first walking stride with front foot right, rear foot left, arms counter-swing. Bottom-left: opposite walking stride, alternate legs and arms. Bottom-right: airborne jump pose with bent knees and raised hands. Keep character identical scale, each pose centered horizontally and vertically in its cell, feet on the same baseline 82 percent down each cell for the three grounded poses; jump is tucked but centered, not displaced. Include whole long tail in each cell. Constraints: EXACTLY FOUR poses, same character proportions and pencil weight, transparent actual background, NO shadow or ground under feet, NO text, no labels, no dividers. Designed to cross-fade between frames in Bevy.
